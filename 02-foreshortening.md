# Drill 2: Foreshortening

**Goal:** Show depth with size and overlap before adding anatomy.

Use [Forward open-hand reach in perspective](https://dynamicposes.art/poses/dp-071-forward-open-hand-reach-in-perspective) and [Reaching upward toward a high camera](https://dynamicposes.art/poses/dp-073-reaching-upward-toward-a-high-camera). Both references place parts of the figure at noticeably different distances from the viewer.

## First pass: 30 seconds per pose

1. Choose the form nearest to the viewer and draw it as one simple mass.
2. Place the torso behind it. Use only a few lines to connect them.
3. Compare the apparent sizes of the near and far forms before drawing contours.

Do not start with fingers or small muscle shapes. The near-to-far order is the subject of this pass.

## Second pass: 2 minutes per pose

Block the shoulder, upper arm, forearm, and hand as overlapping volumes where visible. Check where one form hides another. Draw through a form lightly if you need to understand how it connects, then keep only the visible edges.

## Review

- Which form reads as nearest at a glance?
- Does the arm still connect clearly to the shoulder?
- Did a contour or detail accidentally flatten the depth?

Try one more 30-second drawing from memory before checking the image again. Note the part you could not reconstruct in the [practice log](practice-log.md).
