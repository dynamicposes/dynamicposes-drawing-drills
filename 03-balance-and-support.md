# Drill 3: Balance and support

**Goal:** Make the figure's weight believable by finding its contacts with the ground.

Use [Half-kneeling torso turn](https://dynamicposes.art/poses/dp-012-half-kneeling-torso-turn) and [All-fours support from the side](https://dynamicposes.art/poses/dp-017-all-fours-support-from-the-side). Both are useful for comparing the torso with a supported base.

## First pass: 30 seconds per pose

1. Put a small mark at each visible hand, knee, or foot contact.
2. Place the pelvis and rib cage as two masses above those contacts.
3. Connect the masses and contacts with simple limb directions.

If a contact is hidden, do not invent it. Use the visible silhouette and overlaps to make your best structural guess.

## Second pass: 2 minutes per pose

For the kneeling pose, compare the direction of the shoulders with the pelvis. For the four-point pose, study the space beneath the torso and how the visible supports meet the body. Add only the contours that help explain the weight.

## Review

- Could the figure stay balanced on the contacts you drew?
- Which torso mass seems to carry more of the action?
- Where did you need to guess because the reference hides a connection?

Write your most uncertain observation in the [practice log](practice-log.md); it gives you a useful target for the next session.
