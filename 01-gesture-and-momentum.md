# Drill 1: Gesture and momentum

**Goal:** Make the body's direction readable before drawing details.

Use [Forward leap with long trailing leg](https://dynamicposes.art/poses/dp-040-forward-leap-with-long-trailing-leg) and [Curved running turn](https://dynamicposes.art/poses/dp-034-curved-running-turn). The first reference opens the body into a long airborne shape; the second asks you to keep a turn readable through overlapping limbs.

## First pass: 30 seconds per pose

1. Draw one line for the main direction through the torso and pelvis.
2. Mark the head, rib cage, and pelvis with simple shapes.
3. Add one line for each limb. Leave fingers, facial features, and clothing folds out.

For the leap, look for the difference between the compact leading leg and the extended trailing leg. For the running turn, compare the torso lean with the direction of the legs.

## Second pass: 2 minutes per pose

Draw each pose again. Connect the limbs to the pelvis and shoulders, then check the empty spaces between the arms, legs, and torso. Step back from the drawing: can someone understand the action from its silhouette alone?

## Review

- Is the overall direction visible without the reference beside it?
- Did the rib cage and pelvis keep their relationship as you added limbs?
- Which overlap made the action harder to read?

Record one answer in the [practice log](practice-log.md). If the gesture became stiff in the second pass, repeat the pose with fewer lines.
